Speak naturally

Choose your language and talk. Live Translate translates your audio for participants who use a different language.

Follow the conversation

Read translated captions in your chosen language while you listen and connect with the people in your room.

Connect with a link

Invite others to your video room with a shared link. Turn on your camera and microphone when you are ready.

One conversation. Many languages.

When the Live Translate app is running, getting together takes just a few steps:

  1. Create a session in the app and share the room link.
  2. Choose your language for speaking and listening.
  3. Enable your microphone and start the conversation.

Powered by LiveKit and the Gemini Live API.
